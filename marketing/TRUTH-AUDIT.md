# AlexBET Truth Audit

## Goal
Keep all public messaging for AlexBET accurate, defensible, and useful.

## What AlexBET Lite can honestly claim today
- Tracks bets manually in a clean web app.
- Stores pick, sport, date, odds, edge, stake, result, P&L, CLV, and bankroll context.
- Shows analytics like win rate, P&L, average CLV, ROI, and bankroll drawdown.
- Includes a pending-bets Live Board with richer game cards and score matching logic.
- Shows build/version metadata in the UI so live deploys can be verified.

## What AlexBET Sharp Bot can honestly claim today
- Runs as a Telegram bot.
- Can scan sportsbook odds feeds and return ranked betting ideas.
- Can collect bankroll input and present bankroll-aware bet sizing.
- Can serve as an onboarding funnel for AlexBET Lite and the ebook.
- Has been improved from a random-edge prototype toward a real v1 EV-based scan.

## Claims that are reasonable but should be phrased carefully
Use soft phrasing, not hard promises.

- "Data-driven betting workflow"
- "Helps bettors track CLV, bankroll, and edge"
- "Real-time betting tools"
- "EV-based scan logic"
- "Built for disciplined bettors"
- "Terminal-style workflow for tracking and validation"

## Claims to avoid right now
Do not post these publicly unless product changes make them true.

- "Guaranteed profitable"
- "Proven winning picks"
- "AI predicts winners"
- "Sharp model used by pros"
- "Fully automated sharp betting engine"
- "Best line scanner across all sportsbooks" (too strong)
- "Institutional-grade" (too strong)
- "Real-time alert engine" unless reliability is verified end-to-end
- "Set and forget betting" 

## Recommended public positioning
AlexBET helps bettors move from random picks to a disciplined process.

That process is about:
- tracking CLV
- managing bankroll
- validating edge
- reviewing pending bets and outcomes
- learning from results over time

## Safe one-line positioning
- AlexBET is a betting workflow for disciplined bettors.
- Track your bets, CLV, bankroll, and process in one place.
- Built to help bettors validate edge, not chase hype.

## Product truth summary
### Lite
A clean bet tracking and validation product.

### Bot
An onboarding and odds-scanning companion, not a magic prediction engine.

### Brand
Sell process, validation, discipline, and data. Do not sell fantasy certainty.
