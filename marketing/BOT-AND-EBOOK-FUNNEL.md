# AlexBET Bot + Ebook Funnel

## Funnel structure
X post -> AlexBET bot -> ebook delivery -> AlexBET Lite -> future premium path

## Why this funnel works
- one clear CTA
- lower friction than multiple links
- bot becomes the conversion hub
- ebook gives value before asking for anything larger

Welcome to AlexBET.

This is a betting workflow built for disciplined bettors.

Inside, you can:
- get the free ebook
- scan betting ideas
- track your process with AlexBET Lite

Choose where you want to start.

### Message 2: options
Buttons:
- Get the free ebook
- Open AlexBET Lite
- Scan betting ideas
- Learn how AlexBET works

### Message 3: ebook delivery
Here’s the free AlexBET ebook.

It covers:
- why most bettors fail
- CLV basics
- bankroll discipline
- process over picks
- how to track if your edge is real

After you read it, open AlexBET Lite and start logging your bets.

### Message 4: Lite bridge
AlexBET Lite helps you:
- log picks
- track CLV
- monitor bankroll
- review pending bets
- validate long-term performance

### Message 5: scan bridge
The bot can surface betting ideas and sizing context, but the goal is not blind tailing.
The goal is to build a more disciplined process.
